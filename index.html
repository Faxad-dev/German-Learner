🔢 The German Inversion Rule: Counting 21 to 99

Compound Architecture

In German, numbers from 21 to 99 are spoken ones first, then tens with "und" (and) in the middle. For example: 21 is einundzwanzig (literally: "one-and-twenty"), 22 is zweiundzwanzig ("two-and-twenty"), and 35 is fünfunddreißig ("five-and-thirty").

German Cases Inflection Table

Notice how masculine changes from "der" to "den" in Akkusativ, and "dem" in Dativ.

Case Masc Fem Neut Plural
Nominativ (Subject) der / ein die / eine das / ein die / -
Akkusativ (Direct Obj) den / einen ⚠️ die / eine das / ein die / -
Dativ (Indirect Obj) dem / einem der / einer dem / einem den (+n) / -
💡 Verb Kicker Rule: In clauses with subordinating conjunctions like weil (because) or obwohl (although), the conjugated verb always kicks to the very end of the clause!
